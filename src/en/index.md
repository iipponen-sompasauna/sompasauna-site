# A free sauna by the sea. Always open.

<p class="lead">No staff, no entry fee, no bookings. Sompasauna is kept running
by volunteers on the shore of Sompasaari – come and throw the löyly yourself.</p>

## Nine rules

<ol class="rules">
  <li><div><strong>Sompasauna is always open.</strong> There is no staff – you heat the sauna yourself.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>The sauna is free of charge.</strong> If you wish, you can donate or join the association.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>A mixed sauna for everyone.</strong> There are no separate shifts for women or men.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>Clothing is optional.</strong> You can bathe and swim naked or in a swimsuit.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>Bring drinking water and a towel.</strong> There is no running water, no showers, no washing facilities.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>Bring your own padlock.</strong> There are no changing rooms; there are lockers for valuables.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>Zero tolerance for harassment.</strong> Troublemakers can be banned from the sauna. Report harassment via the SafeSompis form.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>No photography, no speakers.</strong> Acoustic music and singing are welcome – there is even a piano.</div></li>
  <li><div><strong>Don't be stupid.</strong> Support Sompasauna. Every euro keeps us warm.</div></li>
</ol>
